About Me

Dr. Sheikh Marufuzzaman is a Cardiologist with over 40 years of experience in cardiovascular care. He completed his MBBS and MD in Cardiology and currently serves as an Associate Professor of Cardiology at Cumilla Medical College & Hospital.
